Welcome to Lilium House

Whether you choose the timeless charm of Rome or the peaceful landscapes of Crete, each Lilium House apartment is carefully curated to offer a calm, elegant, and residential experience.

Our Philosophy

Make Your Stay Truly Personal

Lilium House is not a hotel. It is a collection of homes created for those who value space, quiet, and authenticity. Each stay is designed to feel effortless, intimate, and deeply connected to its surroundings.

From the natural light that fills each room to the carefully chosen furnishings and local touches, every detail has been considered to create an atmosphere of calm and understated elegance.
