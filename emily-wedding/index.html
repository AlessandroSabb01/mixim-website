Useful Information

What the Weather's Like

Late June in Rome is warm (82–91°F / 28–33°C). Light fabrics, a fan, and sunscreen are your best friends.

Kids

If you need a nanny for the events, you'll find everything in the “Kids & Private Transfers” section.

Gifts

Having you with us in Rome is the greatest gift. If you'd still like to give something, we'll share details closer to the date.

Getting There

The main airports are Fiumicino (FCO) and Ciampino (CIA). For a private airport transfer, see the number under “Private Transfers”. Also note that on Sunday a round-trip shuttle will run between the Tempio Maggiore and Castello Odescalchi (see “Schedule”).
