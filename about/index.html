About

The Story of Lilium House

A collection of homes shaped by calm, place, and personal travel.

A Way of Feeling at Home

Lilium House was born from a simple idea: travel feels better when a place truly feels like home. Each Lilium House property is designed as a personal space rather than an accommodation, offering comfort, privacy, and a strong connection to its surroundings.

From the quiet streets of Rome’s Aventino district to the open landscapes of eastern Crete, Lilium House reflects a Mediterranean way of living — calm, authentic, and unhurried.

What Defines a Lilium House

01

Residential, Not Hotel

Our homes are real residences, designed for living rather than brief overnight stays. Each space has a kitchen, a comfortable workspace, and room to breathe.

02

Natural Light & Space

Generous rooms filled with Mediterranean warmth and breathing room.

03

Calm Neighborhoods

Carefully chosen locations away from tourist crowds, close to real life.

04

Local Connection

Thoughtful recommendations to experience each place authentically.

05

Independent Living

Full kitchens, comfortable workspaces, and everything you need.

06

Slow Travel Philosophy

Designed for those who prefer depth over rushing through destinations.
