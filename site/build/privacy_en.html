2. What we collect and why

Consultation requests

When you use the booking form, or write to us by email or WhatsApp, we process the details you give us: name, email address, company or brand, website or social profile, the topics you are interested in, your preferred meeting format and your message.

  • Purpose: to answer your request, arrange and hold the consultation, send you payment details for it and, if you wish, prepare a project proposal.

  • Retention: if we do not start working together, we delete your request within 24 months of our last contact. If you become a client, we keep contract and invoicing records for as long as tax and accounting law requires (generally 10 years).

Technical data when you visit

Our hosting provider automatically records standard server logs (IP address, date and time, page requested, browser and device type) to keep the website secure and running. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in a secure, working website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). These logs are kept for a short period and then deleted by the provider.

3. Who receives your data

We never sell your data. We only share it with service providers who help us run the studio and who process it on our behalf under a data processing agreement:

  • Website hosting: Hostinger International Ltd. (Cyprus, EU).
  • Email and form storage: Google Ireland Ltd. (Google Workspace / Gmail / Google Sheets). Google may process data in the United States; such transfers rely on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.
  • Payments for the consultation: the payment provider indicated in our reply (for example a bank transfer or a card payment page), which acts as an independent controller for the payment.
  • Online booking: Cal.com, Inc. (United States) provides our booking calendar. When you click a booking button, the calendar loads from Cal.com and the details you enter (name, email, notes, chosen time) are processed by Cal.com on our behalf, on the basis of the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses. Cal.com may set the cookies it needs to run the calendar.
  • Payment for the consultation: Stripe Payments Europe, Ltd. (Ireland) processes the €50 card payment. Card details go directly to Stripe and are never seen or stored by us; Stripe acts as an independent controller for payment and fraud-prevention data.

If you choose to contact us on WhatsApp, WhatsApp Ireland Ltd. processes that conversation under its own privacy policy.

4. Cookies and similar technologies

This website does not use advertising or profiling cookies, and it does not load fonts, scripts or images from third parties: everything is served from our own server. The only exception is the Cal.com booking calendar, which loads only after you click a booking button.

We store two small preferences in your browser's local storage, which are strictly necessary and therefore do not require consent: your chosen language (mx-lang) and, where applicable, your cookie choice (mx-consent).

Analytics (only with your consent). If you click "Accept analytics", we load Google Analytics 4 (Google Ireland Ltd.) to understand, in aggregate, how visitors use the site. It sets the cookies _ga and _ga_* (up to 13 months). Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and Art. 5(3) of the ePrivacy Directive). Nothing is loaded before you accept, and you can withdraw consent at any time via "Cookie settings" in the footer; we then delete those cookies.
