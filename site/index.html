Questions

Before we meet.

Why is there no price list?

Because no two brands need the same thing. After the consultation you receive a written plan with a clear investment for exactly what you need, nothing more and nothing less.

What happens during the consultation?

One focused hour about your brand, your goals and what is holding you back. You leave with first insights; we leave knowing enough to write your plan.

What does the consultation cost?

The hour costs €50. If you choose to work with us, the full €50 is credited to your project.

Can we order a single service?

Of course. Order one course à la carte, combine a few, or ask us to compose the full table.

Do you work with clients outside Italy?

Yes. We work in English and Italian, in person and remotely, with clients across Europe and beyond.
